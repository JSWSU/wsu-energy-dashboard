DATA RULES USED BY THE PAGE
---
Building electric = 0802_EL_012 (208 V service) + the nine 480 V circuit meters
                    (0802_EL_002 to 009, 011).
0802_EL_001 (480 V main) is left out. On 09/28/2026 it read 124 V line to line while its
circuit meters read 484 to 493 V; its kW reads about one quarter of the circuit sum.
0802_DW_001 (domestic cold water) recorded zero flow from 02/06/2026 to 09/28/2026.
Daily energy = daily average demand x 24. The current partial day is excluded.
Cost = electricity $0.1037/kWh + chilled water $0.1823/ton-hr (FY27 rates, "Utility Rates
for FY27" email 12/22/2025), counted from 07/01/2026. No heating cost.
The stacked weekly chart shows negative readings (0802_EL_007, 0802_EL_008) as zero.
