Words you will see in the data

kW (kilowatt): how fast the building uses electricity right now.
kWh (kilowatt-hour): an amount of electricity. 1 kW for 1 hour = 1 kWh.
BTU/h: how fast heat moves. 12,000 BTU/h = 1 ton of cooling.
kBtu: 1,000 BTU. 1 kWh = 3.412 kBtu, so all energy types fit on one chart.
cfm: cubic feet of air per minute, the airflow into a room.
ppm CO₂: carbon dioxide in the air. People breathe it out. Outdoor air is near 420 ppm.
Set point (sp): the target value the controls try to hold.
Occupied / unoccupied: the schedule mode. Unoccupied mode lets rooms drift to save energy.
Valve or damper %: how far open. 0% is closed, 100% is fully open.
Supply / return: water or air going out to the rooms, and coming back.
Delta T: supply and return temperature difference. Bigger delta T means more heat moved per gallon.
VFD: variable frequency drive. It slows a fan or pump motor down to save energy.
